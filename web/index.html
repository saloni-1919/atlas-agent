Local-first by design

Know where your information goes

Documents, chats and approvals are stored in atlas.sqlite on your computer. Offline mode makes no AI service requests. Ollama runs on your PC. OpenAI mode sends selected source excerpts to its API.

This demonstration is designed for one local user. Do not expose it to the internet or upload confidential business documents.

Quick reference

Use /calc (12+8)*2 in chat to propose a calculation. Approve it manually in Action center. Local AI setup instructions and backup/migration steps are in the included README.
